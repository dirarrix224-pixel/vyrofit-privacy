VYRO FIT • OFFICIAL PRIVACY POLICY

Privacy Policy & Data Protection

Application: VYRO FIT | Package Name: com.vyrofit.app | Effective Date: October 27, 2026

1. Introduction & Publisher Identity

Welcome to VYRO FIT (referred to as "the App" or "we"). This application is developed and operated by independent publisher Dirar (VYRO FIT Team).

We are firmly committed to safeguarding your personal privacy and adhering to international data protection principles, including the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), and official Google Play Developer Distribution Policies.

2. Categories of Data You Input

While utilizing the workout tracking and nutrition tools within the App, you may optionally input the following information:

  • Physical Attributes: Body weight, height, age, biological sex, and general physical activity level.
  • Workout Logs: Completed exercises, set counts, rep repetitions, weight lifted in kilograms, rest intervals, and Personal Records (PR).
  • Nutrition & Calories: Daily calorie targets, estimated food/meal logs, and macronutrient intake.

3. Local Storage via Room Database (No Account Required)

100% Local Device Storage: All biometric metrics, workout sets, custom training routines, and calorie logs are saved solely on your physical device using the sandboxed, encrypted Android Room SQLite database.

The application requires no user account registration, no social login, and no disclosure of real-name identity or personal email addresses to utilize all primary fitness functions.

4. Absence of External Proprietary Cloud Servers

VYRO FIT does not maintain proprietary cloud servers to harvest, host, monetize, or transmit your physical, health, or workout records. Your private gym routines and biometric metrics remain strictly on your local hardware.

5. Google AdMob & User Messaging Platform (UMP SDK)

The application incorporates Google Mobile Ads SDK and Google User Messaging Platform (UMP SDK) to deliver commercial banner, interstitial, and rewarded ads to support continuous development free of charge:

  • Google Mobile Ads: Google may process device advertising identifiers (Google Advertising ID / GAID), coarse approximate location derived from IP addresses, and diagnostic crash data in compliance with the Google Privacy Policy.
  • UMP Consent Management: Users located within the European Economic Area (EEA), United Kingdom, Switzerland, and California are presented with official UMP consent dialogues to govern ad personalization.
  • Privacy Choices: You can review, revoke, or adjust your advertising consent preferences at any time directly through: Settings > Privacy & Security > Privacy Choices.

6. Purposes of Data Processing

All stored variables are processed strictly on your hardware for:

  • Computing 1-Repetition Maximum (1RM) utilizing the validated Epley formula.
  • Calculating Basal Metabolic Rate (BMR) and Total Daily Energy Expenditure (TDEE).
  • Generating progressive overload recommendations for safe, continuous strength progression.
  • Displaying training calendar heatmaps, weekly volume progression charts, and muscle split distributions.

7. Data Protection & Android Sandboxing

We rely upon Android's native application sandbox security architecture. Other third-party applications installed on your mobile phone cannot access the private SQLite database files belonging to VYRO FIT.

8. Data Retention & Permanent One-Tap Erasure

Full Erasure Available Instantly: You retain complete control over your history. You can permanently wipe all workout logs, sets, and calorie records inside the App via: Settings > Privacy & Security > Delete Local Data.

Alternatively, uninstalling the App or clearing its storage through Android system settings eliminates all database contents permanently.

9. Your Privacy Rights

In accordance with applicable privacy laws, you possess the right to:

  • Inspect your local metrics under Manage My Data.
  • Erase all personal history instantly without restriction.
  • Modify ad personalization consent through Privacy Choices.
